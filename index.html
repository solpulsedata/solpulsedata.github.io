Methodology and limits

Prices come from Raydium V4 pool vaults that are checked on-chain for owner and mint before use. Hourly candles are recorded by SolPulseData's own collector, so history starts when the collector starts. RSI-14 needs 15 candles and SMA-200 needs 200. Until those warmups finish, the fields show null.
